About Spark

Dating that starts in the real world.

Our idea

Most dating apps are endless swiping. Spark starts from where you are: a live map shows who is Available nearby, so a spontaneous coffee, walk or dinner is only a request away.

How we think about safety

  • 18+ only. Everyone confirms their age at sign-up.
  • Approximate location. Other members see a blurred position, never your exact spot.
  • You choose when to be seen. Go Unavailable and you vanish from the map instantly.
  • Consent first. A meeting request is only an invitation – the other person must accept.
  • Preferences are respected. Choose who you'd like to meet and who you'd rather not, with age and distance filters.

About this project

This is a front-end prototype built with HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. Accounts, availability, bookings and the admin panel are simulated in your browser with localStorage – a real launch needs a secure backend, hashed passwords, identity verification and moderation.
