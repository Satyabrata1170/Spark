Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary for this prototype.

What we collect

Name, email, username, password, gender, age, city, bio, interests, optional photo, availability status, booking requests and – only if you allow it – your device location.

Location

  • We ask permission through a pop-up; you can decline and choose a city instead.
  • Your position is shown to others only while you are Available, and only as an approximate point.
  • Set yourself Unavailable, or turn off location sharing in your profile, to disappear.

Who can see what

Visitors who are not logged in see blurred markers only. Members see the profiles of Available people nearby. Administrators can view and manage member accounts to keep the community safe.

Where data is stored

In this prototype everything is stored in your own browser (localStorage) and never sent to a server. A production version must use encrypted storage, hashed passwords and a proper privacy review.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your profile at any time from My Profile.
  • Request deletion of your account – deleting it removes your profile and bookings.
  • Contact us through the contact page.

Age requirement

Spark is for adults only (18+). Accounts of anyone found to be under 18 are removed.
